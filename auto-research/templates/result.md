## Question and exact claim

State the bounded question, hypotheses, quantifiers, conclusion, and falsification criterion. Distinguish the desired claim from what this result actually establishes.

## Approach and derivation

Give enough definitions, reasoning, and source references for a reader to check each step. Identify any unproved assumptions and required bridges to the same Yang–Mills hierarchy.

## Reproduction and evidence

Record commands from a stated working directory, input data/seeds, environment, resource use where relevant, exit codes, and artifact/log links. Separate historical output from new execution. For Lean, include the exact theorem statement, admissions/axiom report, toolchain, dependencies, and extent of replay. For numerical work, include uncertainty and limitations. State when no execution occurred.

## Outcome and limits

Explain exactly what passed, failed, or remains unknown. A failed attempt is not an impossibility theorem. Label toy-model scope and state which Yang–Mills hypotheses were not established.

## Failure lesson or revised expectation

For negative results: what went wrong, the unsupported inference or missing hypothesis, what agents should always check instead, and evidence that could justify reopening the route. For other results: state any new obligation or limitation discovered. Link a prompt update if one is warranted.

## Track contributions and next experiment

List affected tracks and the concrete milestone or next question changed by this result. Propose a bounded next attempt, its success criterion, dependencies, and estimated cost when known. Record blockers honestly.

## Review and correction history

Append dated reviews, corrections, and superseding links. Preserve the original evidence and explain any corrected current claim.
